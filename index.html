AOW & pension off

In today's money; both rise with inflation. Dividends must cover your costs minus these; until they start you sell shares for that part (the bridge). Your AOW age depends on life expectancy (about 68 for people born around 1990: check svb.nl). Set an amount to 0 to leave it out.

Investments

While working (recurring ETF)

In retirement (living on dividends)

Dividends are reinvested while you work. At retirement you switch to the retirement ETF; the Netherlands has no capital gains tax, so switching costs only fees. S&P 500 figures are 2005–2024 and 1957–2024 averages, before inflation and fund costs.

Costs

Fund cost (TER) comes off the growth. Dividend leakage is the share of dividends lost to withholding tax inside the fund that you can't claim back: about 10–15% for Irish-domiciled world and US ETFs.

Box 3 tax

Old box 3 (deemed return)

New box 3 (actual return)

Defaults are the 2026 rules (6.00% deemed return, €59,357 tax-free) and the new-system bill. Tax-free amounts and your monthly cost rise with inflation. Dividend withholding tax is assumed to be credited.

Mortgage

Interest deduction (hypotheekrenteaftrek)

You get back deduction rate × (interest − WOZ × forfait) each year. The WOZ value grows by its own rate (4% ≈ long-run Dutch house prices), so the forfait rises and the refund shrinks. Defaults are 2026: 37.56% and 0.35%. It only applies while you earn a salary: after you retire on dividends there is no income tax left to deduct from. Set the rate to 0 to leave it out.
